Bound Demon

Numbers are computed from the game's data tables (skills, monstats, monlvl, monpet) as extracted for patch 3.3 by blizzhackers/d2data. The game does not show minion sheets, so totals are best estimates: flat bonuses are added to the monster's base value and percentages applied afterwards.

Bound demon values cover its melee attack only — its own spells, auras and champion/unique damage bonuses are not modelled. In Nightmare and Hell the monster level is the area level (+2 champion, +3 unique or minion); adjust it for the zone you bound it in.
